# 社区协作约定 Community conduct

请围绕问题和证据开展讨论，尊重不同背景、能力和观点的参与者。欢迎具体、可执行的批评；不接受骚扰、歧视、人身攻击、威胁、冒充他人或未经许可公开个人与企业敏感信息。

维护者可以要求修改不合适的内容、关闭偏离主题的讨论，或限制持续违反约定的参与。处理时应说明原因并保护举报人隐私。如遇相关问题，请通过[维护者主页](https://github.com/xydzf520)联系维护者，不在公开讨论中传播敏感证据。

Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Constructive criticism is welcome; harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, impersonation and unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information are not. Maintainers may request edits, close off-topic discussions or restrict repeated violations, explaining their actions while protecting privacy. Contact the [maintainer](https://github.com/xydzf520) about concerns without posting sensitive evidence publicly.
